UNIT 19 Reported Speech 📖 8 Latihan

Kalimat tidak langsung (Reported Speech)

Reported Speech
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Kalimat tidak langsung digunakan untuk menceritakan kembali ucapan orang lain. Terjadi perubahan tenses (mundur satu tingkat ke masa lampau), kata ganti orang, dan penanda waktu.

Perubahan Tense (Tense Backshift)
Kalimat Langsung (Direct)Kalimat Tidak Langsung (Reported)
Present Simple (am/is/are, V1)Past Simple (was/were, V2)
Present Continuous (am/is/are + V-ing)Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing)
will + V1would + V1
can + V1could + V1
Perbedaan say vs tell
Kata KerjaAturan PenggunaanContoh
sayTidak langsung diikuti orang (objek)He said (that) he was tired.
tellWajib langsung diikuti orang (objek)He told ME (that) he was tired.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Perubahan penanda waktu: now -> then, today -> that day, tomorrow -> the next day, yesterday -> the day before.
  • Perubahan kata tunjuk: this -> that, these -> those, here -> there.
