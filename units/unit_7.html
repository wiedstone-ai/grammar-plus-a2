could (Kemampuan Masa Lalu & Kesopanan)

could: Past Ability, Polite Requests, Possibility
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

'Could' digunakan untuk menyatakan kemampuan umum di masa lampau, meminta bantuan dengan santun, atau menyatakan kemungkinan.

Fungsi could
FungsiPolaContoh
Past AbilitySubject + could + V1I could swim when I was six.
Polite RequestCould you + V1...?Could you help me with this box?
Asking PermissionCould I + V1...?Could I use your phone, please?
PossibilitySubject + could + V1It could be very hot tomorrow.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Bentuk negatif dari could adalah couldn't (could not).
  • Selalu diikuti kata kerja bentuk pertama tanpa 'to'.
