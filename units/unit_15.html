UNIT 15 Verb Patterns 📖 10 Latihan

Kata kerja + infinitif; kata kerja + ing

Verbs followed by Infinitive or -ing (Gerund)
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Ketika dua kata kerja berdampingan, kata kerja kedua bisa berbentuk to-infinitive (to do) atau gerund (-ing) tergantung kata kerja utamanya.

Daftar Kata Kerja dan Polanya
PolaKata Kerja UmumContoh Kalimat
Verb + to-infinitivewant, hope, decide, plan, promise, learn, offerI decided to study abroad.
Verb + -ing (Gerund)enjoy, mind, avoid, finish, practice, imagineShe enjoys playing the guitar.
Preposition + -inggood at, interested in, keen on, tired ofHe is good at swimming.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Setelah kata depan (preposisi: at, in, of, about, etc.), kata kerja SELALU berakhiran -ing.
  • Beberapa kata kerja seperti like, love, hate dapat diikuti keduanya dengan makna serupa.
