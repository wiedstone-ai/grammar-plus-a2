UNIT 3 Communication Skills 📖 10 Latihan

Mengajak, Menerima, Menolak (Invitations & Suggestions)

Making, Accepting, and Refusing Invitations
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Pola kalimat untuk mengajak seseorang melakukan kegiatan bersama, serta cara merespons ajakan tersebut dengan menerima atau menolak secara sopan.

Ungkapan Mengajak, Menerima, dan Menolak
Mengajak (Inviting/Suggesting)Menerima (Accepting)Menolak (Polite Refusing)
Would you like to + Verb?Yes, I'd love to! / That sounds great!I'm sorry, I can't. I have to study.
Let's + VerbGood idea! / Let's do that!I'd rather not. I'm really tired.
How about + Verb-ing?Sure, why not? / Great!I'm afraid I'm busy then.
Why don't we + Verb?That would be wonderful!Thanks for asking, but I have other plans.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • 'Would you like to' dan 'Why don't we' selalu diikuti kata kerja dasar (infinitive): 'Would you like to go to the cinema?'
  • 'How about' dan 'What about' SELALU diikuti oleh bentuk kata kerja -ing atau kata benda: 'How about having pizza?'
  • Saat menolak ajakan dalam bahasa Inggris, biasakan selalu diawali permohonan maaf dan alasan yang masuk akal agar tetap sopan.
