Present perfect continuous

Present Perfect Continuous
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Digunakan untuk menyatakan kegiatan yang telah dimulai di masa lalu dan masih terus berlangsung hingga saat ini, atau baru saja berhenti dengan bukti yang jelas saat ini.

Rumus Present Perfect Continuous
BentukRumusContoh
PositifSubject + have/has been + V-ingIt has been raining for two hours.
NegatifSubject + have/has not been + V-ingI haven't been sleeping well lately.
TanyaHow long + have/has + Subject + been + V-ing?How long have you been studying English?
Perbedaan for vs since
Kata DepanFungsiContoh
forMenunjukkan durasi / rentang waktufor two hours, for three days, for ten years
sinceMenunjukkan titik awal waktu dimulaisince 8 a.m., since Monday, since 2015, since yesterday
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Pertanyaan mengenai durasi kegiatan menggunakan 'How long have you been...?'
  • Kata kerja statis (state verbs seperti know, like, believe) umumnya menggunakan Present Perfect Simple, bukan Continuous.
