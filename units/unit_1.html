Kata Sifat dan Kata Ganti Posesif; Whose

Possessive Adjectives & Pronouns; Whose
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Kata sifat posesif (Possessive Adjectives) digunakan sebelum kata benda untuk menyatakan kepemilikan. Sedangkan kata ganti posesif (Possessive Pronouns) berdiri sendiri menggantikan kata benda tersebut.

Tabel Kata Sifat & Kata Ganti Posesif
SubjekKata Sifat Posesif (Adjective)Kata Ganti Posesif (Pronoun)Contoh
ImymineThis is my moped. It's mine.
YouyouryoursThat's your book. It's yours.
HehishisThis is his camera. It's his.
SheherhersThat's her phone. It's hers.
Itits-The cat ate its food.
WeouroursThese are our seats. They're ours.
You (plural)youryoursAre these your pencils? Are they yours?
TheytheirtheirsThose are their coats. They're theirs.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Kata sifat posesif SELALU diletakkan sebelum kata benda: 'This is my sister\'s tablet.'
  • Kata ganti posesif berdiri sendiri dan TIDAK diikuti kata benda: 'That\'s her mobile. = It\'s hers.'
  • Gunakan 'whose' untuk menanyakan kepemilikan: 'Whose bag is this? It\'s mine.'
  • Ingat! Jangan tertukar antara 'whose' (milik siapa) dengan 'who\'s' (= who is atau who has).
