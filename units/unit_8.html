UNIT 8 Quantifiers & Determiners 📖 4 Latihan

some, any, no, every

Quantifiers: some, any, no, every
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Kata penentu jumlah (quantifiers) yang digunakan bersama kata benda dapat dihitung (countable) maupun tidak dapat dihitung (uncountable).

Aturan some, any, no, every
KataJenis KalimatContoh
someKalimat Positif (+), Tawaran/Permintaan SopanI have some apples. Would you like some tea?
anyKalimat Negatif (-), Kalimat Tanya umum (?)I don't have any money. Do you have any pens?
noBermakna negatif tapi dengan kata kerja positif (+)There are no chairs in the room.
everyDiikuti kata benda tunggal (singular countable)Every student has a dictionary.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Jangan gunakan 'no' bersama kalimat yang sudah ada 'not' (Double negative salah: BUKAN 'I don't have no time').
  • 'Every' selalu diikuti kata benda tunggal: 'every student', bukan 'every students'.
