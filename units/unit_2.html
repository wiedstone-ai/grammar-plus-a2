Penggunaan have, can, dan could

Usage of have, can, and could
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Kata kerja 'have' sering digunakan dalam kolokasi kegiatan sehari-hari. 'Can' digunakan untuk menyatakan kemampuan, meminta izin, atau kemungkinan di masa sekarang. 'Could' digunakan untuk kemampuan di masa lampau dan permintaan yang lebih sopan.

Kolokasi Umum Menggunakan have
UngkapanArti / MaknaContoh Kalimat
have breakfast / lunch / dinnerMakan pagi / siang / malamI have breakfast at 7 a.m.
have a snack / a mealMakan cemilan / hidanganLet's have a snack.
have a shower / bathMandi shower / berendamShe has a warm shower.
have a rest / holidayIstirahat / liburanWe had a wonderful holiday.
have a chat / an argumentMengobrol / bertengkarThey're having a quick chat.
have a dreamBermimpiI had a strange dream last night.
Penggunaan can dan could
ModalFungsiContoh
canKemampuan saat ini (ability)I can play the piano.
canMeminta izin / tawaran (permission/offer)Can I borrow this book?
canKemungkinan (possibility)You can buy sun cream here.
couldKemampuan masa lalu (past ability)He could swim when he was 8.
couldPermintaan lebih sopan (polite request)Could you pass the salt, please?
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Jangan tertukar antara 'have' (kegiatan) dengan 'have got' (kepemilikan fisik). Bentuk negatif 'have' menggunakan don't/doesn't/didn't: 'She doesn't have a shower in the morning.'
  • 'can' dan 'could' TIDAK pernah berubah bentuk (tidak ada 'cans' atau 'coulds').
  • 'can' dan 'could' langsung diikuti oleh kata kerja dasar (infinitive without to): 'I can sing.' (BUKAN 'I can to sing').
