UNIT 14 Sentence Structure 📖 10 Latihan

Klausa penghubung (Relative Clauses)

Relative Clauses: who, which, that, where, whose
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Klausa penghubung memberikan informasi tambahan tentang orang, benda, kepemilikan, atau tempat tanpa harus membuat kalimat baru.

Kata Ganti Relatif (Relative Pronouns)
Kata RelatifDigunakan UntukContoh Kalimat
whoOrang (People)The girl who won the race is my sister.
whichBenda / Hewan (Things/Animals)The book which is on the table is mine.
thatOrang atau Benda (Informal/Defining)The laptop that I bought is fast.
whereTempat (Places)This is the restaurant where we had dinner.
whoseKepemilikan (Possession)I met a boy whose father is an astronaut.
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • 'who' menggantikan he/she/they.
  • 'whose' menggantikan his/her/their untuk kepemilikan.
  • 'where' menggantikan there / at that place.
