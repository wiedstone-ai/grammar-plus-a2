Pertanyaan dan jawaban singkat (Question Tags)

Question Tags & Short Answers
📖 Ringkasan Rumus & Materi Tata Bahasa ▼

Question tag adalah pertanyaan pendek di ujung kalimat yang dipakai untuk meminta konfirmasi atau persetujuan lawan bicara.

Pola Question Tag
PernyataanQuestion TagContoh Kalimat
Kalimat Positif (+)Tag Negatif (-)You are a student, aren't you?
Kalimat Negatif (-)Tag Positif (+)You don't smoke, do you?
Modal can (+)Tag can't (-)She can speak Spanish, can't she?
Past Simple (+)Tag didn't (-)Tom arrived late, didn't he?
Aturan & Catatan Penting:
  • Kasus khusus: 'I am right, aren't I?'
  • Kalimat ajakan: 'Let's go, shall we?'
  • Perintah: 'Open the window, will you?'
